01 Attack demo

The router can cheat.

A toothache question becomes a sponsored recommendation or an answer from the wrong model. Our demo exposes the router actions behind these outcomes.

02 Verification demo

Make the router prove it.

Our framework, VeriRouter, uses zero-knowledge proofs to verify model eligibility and endorsed request rewriting. A provider-signed serving certificate authenticates the model and its answer. The router’s semantic index and rewrite banks stay private.
